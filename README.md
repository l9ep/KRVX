# KRVX

Make your videos sharp. TikTok and Instagram won't ruin them.

KRVX processes your videos entirely on your device — nothing gets uploaded anywhere. Open it, drop your file, pick a method, done.

---

## What it does

Takes your video and re-encodes it at a higher quality so it survives platform compression. When you upload to TikTok or Reels, they crush your video to save bandwidth. KRVX outputs at a high enough quality and bitrate that even after their compression, it still looks clean.

---

## Methods

**Crisp** — 1440p at 120fps. Fastest to process. No quality compromise, just gets it done quick.

**TikTok / Reels** — 4K at 60fps, 50Mbps. The default. Built specifically for short clips going on TikTok or Instagram Reels.

**Balanced** — 2K at 60fps, 25Mbps. Good quality with a smaller output file. Best when you need to send or upload somewhere with a size limit.

**Ultra** — 4K at 60fps, 80Mbps. Near-lossless. Use this when quality is everything and file size doesn't matter.

---

## How to use

1. Drop your video or tap Browse
2. Pick a method
3. Hit Upscale
4. Preview the result and save

That's it. No account, no upload, no install.

---

## Works with

MP4, MOV, AVI, WEBM, MKV

---

## Notes

- Everything runs locally. Your video never leaves your device.
- Large files (500MB+) may be slow depending on your device.
- Works on mobile — tap Browse to pick from your gallery or files app.
- Requires a modern browser with WebAssembly support (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
