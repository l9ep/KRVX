# KRVX

Upscale your videos so TikTok and Instagram don't destroy the quality when you upload.

---

## What it does

When you upload a video to TikTok or Instagram, they compress it automatically. That compression kills sharpness, introduces blur, and makes everything look worse than the original. KRVX processes your video before you upload it — bumping the resolution up to 4K and cranking the quality settings so high that even after the platform compresses it, the result still looks clean.

---

## How it works

You drop in a video. KRVX runs it through a full re-encode using a sharp upscaling filter called Lanczos, which is known for preserving edge detail when resizing. It then outputs an MP4 in H.264 format — the codec both TikTok and Instagram handle best — with near-lossless quality settings. The whole thing runs in your browser, nothing gets uploaded anywhere.

---

## Settings

**Output Resolution** — How big the output video will be. 4K is the default and gives the best results after platform compression. You can go lower if file size is a concern.

**Quality** — A number between 12 and 28. Lower means sharper and bigger file. 15 is the default and is basically lossless to the eye.

**Sharpening** — Max Sharp uses Lanczos which keeps edges crisp. Smooth and Fast are softer and process quicker.

---

## On mobile

Tap Browse. Your phone will let you pick from your gallery or files app depending on your OS. After processing finishes, tap Download and the file saves to your device. Then upload that file directly to TikTok or Instagram.

---

## Notes

Processing happens entirely on your device. No servers, no uploads, no accounts. Bigger files will take longer depending on your device's speed. The output file will be larger than the input — that's intentional, more data means more survives compression.
